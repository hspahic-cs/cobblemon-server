Balance Beginner

Zapdos · Mega Scizor

The dependable all-rounder, and the best team to learn on. It punishes greedy play and forgives your mistakes — if a plan falls apart, you almost always have an answer in the back. If it's your first rental, start here.

How to play
  1. Lead Garchomp and set Stealth Rock — those entry hazards chip every Pokémon they switch in.
  2. Keep the momentum: U-turn or Volt Switch to pivot into whatever beats their current Pokémon.
  3. Grind them down — let hazards and chip do the work, and heal with Roost / Recover when you dip low.
  4. Close it out: once their answers are gone, boost with Swords Dance Scizor or Nasty Plot Gholdengo and sweep.
  5. Facing a scary setup sweeper? Dondozo ignores their stat boosts and walls them cold.

Gimmick: Trick Room Moderate

Cresselia · Mega Mawile

Break the rules of speed. For five turns, Trick Room flips the battle so your slowest Pokémon move first — and those slow Pokémon hit like a freight train. Completely different from every other team, and a blast once it clicks.

How to play
  1. Lead a setter (Cresselia or Hatterene) and click Trick Room — now your slow Pokémon move first.
  2. Immediately bring in a bruiser — Mawile, Ursaluna, or Conkeldurr — and start swinging.
  3. Count your turns: Trick Room lasts 5 turns. Make each one a knockout.
  4. When it's about to end, reset it with your second setter — or sacrifice Cresselia with Lunar Dance to bring a bruiser back at full HP.
  5. Between rooms, Skeledirge is your safety net: it walls hits and heals.

Hyper Offense Hard

Chi-Yu · Mega Charizard X

Pure aggression. You slam down entry hazards on turn one, then send a chain of hard hitters to blow holes in the enemy before they can dig in. One misread can cost you a Pokémon — the biggest risk-and-reward team of the four.

How to play
  1. Lead Glimmora and stack Stealth Rock + Spikes — Focus Sash means you almost always get both layers down.
  2. Pick your breaker: send in the attacker that threatens their team and force a knockout or a switch.
  3. Keep the pressure — U-turn with Dragapult or Meowscarada to bring the right hitter in for free.
  4. Set up when it's safe: a Dragon Dance Charizard X or Swords Dance Kingambit ends games on the spot.
  5. Late game, Kingambit cleans up — it grows stronger for every fainted ally, and Sucker Punch (priority) picks off faster foes.

Stall Expert

Ting-Lu · Mega Sableye

Victory by patience. You bring no sweepers — instead you wall the enemy out, wear them down with status, and heal off everything they throw at you. The deepest skill ceiling of the four: every switch matters, but pilot it well and nothing can touch you.

How to play
  1. Set Stealth Rock with Ting-Lu and keep the entry hazards up all game.
  2. Spread status: Toxic and Will-O-Wisp everything — poisoned and burned foes lose the long game.
  3. Wall the current threat: pivot to whoever best takes its hits — Chansey for special attackers, Corviknight / Gliscor for physical.
  4. Heal off the damage (Recover, Roost, Soft-Boiled) and phaze with Whirlwind to rack up hazard chip.
  5. Win by attrition — as their team crumbles to status and chip, you simply outlast them. Patience takes it.

Ready to build your own? ⚔️

Rentals are training wheels — the real fun is making a team that's yours. A few ways to start:

  • Pick an archetype first. Want to be aggressive, safe, grindy, or gimmicky? The four teams above are templates — copy one and start swapping pieces.
  • Cover your bases. Aim for broad type coverage on offense so you can hit almost anything, and always keep in mind what your team is weak to — if half your Pokémon fold to Ground moves, add something that resists it. And leave an answer for hazards, setup sweepers, and priority.
  • Steal what works. Drop a favourite of yours into one slot, keep the rest, and see how it plays before rebuilding from scratch.
  • Learn what's strong. Smogon's Gen 9 dex lists movesets, roles, and viability rankings for every Pokémon — the best place to see what top players run and why. Browse the SV OU dex →
