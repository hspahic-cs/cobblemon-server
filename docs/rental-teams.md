# Rental Teams

Rentals are ready-made competitive teams you can play instantly — no breeding, no training, no prep. Pick one from the team-select screen and jump straight into ranked. This guide helps you find the team that fits you and shows you how to pilot it.

!!! tip "How to rent"
    Start a ranked battle, click the green **Rent a Team** button on the team-select screen, then pick one of the four teams below. You're locked in instantly.

**Difficulty:** <span class="diff beg">Beginner</span> forgiving · <span class="diff mod">Moderate</span> a twist to learn · <span class="diff hard">Hard</span> high risk/reward · <span class="diff exp">Expert</span> deepest skill ceiling

!!! note "Hover any move"
    Move badges are colored by type. **Hover** (or tap) a move to see its category, power, PP, and what it does.


## Balance <span class="diff beg">Beginner</span>

*Zapdos · Mega Scizor*

The dependable all-rounder, and the best team to learn on. It punishes greedy play and forgives your mistakes — if a plan falls apart, you almost always have an answer waiting in the back. **If it's your first rental, start here.**

**How to play**

1. Lead **Garchomp** and set **Stealth Rock** — those [entry hazards](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Entry_hazard) chip every Pokémon they switch in.
2. Keep the momentum: **U-turn** or **Volt Switch** to pivot into whatever beats their current Pokémon.
3. Grind them down — let hazards and chip do the work, and heal with **Roost** / **Recover** when you dip low.
4. Close it out: once their answers are gone, boost with **Swords Dance** Scizor or **Nasty Plot** Gholdengo and sweep.
5. Facing a scary setup sweeper? **Dondozo** ignores their stat boosts and walls them cold.

## Trick Room <span class="diff mod">Moderate</span>

*Cresselia · Mega Mawile*

Break the rules of speed. For five turns, [Trick Room](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Trick_Room_(move)) flips the battle so your **slowest** Pokémon move first — and those slow Pokémon hit like a freight train. Completely different from every other team, and a blast once it clicks.

**How to play**

1. Lead a setter (**Cresselia** or **Hatterene**) and click **Trick Room** — now your slow Pokémon move first.
2. Immediately bring in a bruiser — **Mawile**, **Ursaluna**, or **Conkeldurr** — and start swinging.
3. Count your turns: Trick Room lasts **5 turns**. Make each one a knockout.
4. When the room is about to end, reset it with your second setter — or sacrifice **Cresselia** with **Lunar Dance** to bring a bruiser back at full HP.
5. Between rooms, **Skeledirge** is your safety net: it walls hits and heals with Slack Off.

## Hyper Offense <span class="diff hard">Hard</span>

*Chi-Yu · Mega Charizard X*

Pure aggression. You slam down [hazards](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Entry_hazard) on turn one, then send in a chain of hard-hitting attackers to blow holes in the enemy team before they can dig in. One misread can cost you a Pokémon — the biggest risk-and-reward team of the four.

**How to play**

1. Lead **Glimmora** and stack **Stealth Rock** + **Spikes** — Focus Sash means you almost always get both layers down.
2. Pick your breaker: send in the attacker that threatens their team and force a knockout or a switch.
3. Keep the pressure — **U-turn** with Dragapult or Meowscarada to bring the right hitter in for free.
4. Set up when it's safe: a **Dragon Dance** Charizard X or **Swords Dance** Kingambit ends games on the spot.
5. Late game, **Kingambit** cleans up — it grows stronger for every fainted ally, and Sucker Punch ([priority](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Priority)) picks off faster foes.

## Stall <span class="diff exp">Expert</span>

*Ting-Lu · Mega Sableye*

Victory by patience. You bring no sweepers — instead you wall the enemy out, wear them down with [status](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Status_condition), and heal off everything they throw at you. It has the deepest skill ceiling of the four: every switch matters, but pilot it well and nothing can touch you.

**How to play**

1. Set **Stealth Rock** with **Ting-Lu** and keep the [hazards](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Entry_hazard) up all game.
2. Spread [status](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Status_condition): **Toxic** and **Will-O-Wisp** everything — poisoned and burned foes lose the long game.
3. Wall the current threat: pivot to whoever best takes its hits — **Chansey** for special attackers, **Corviknight** / **Gliscor** for physical.
4. Heal off the damage (**Recover**, **Roost**, **Soft-Boiled**) and phaze with **Whirlwind** to rack up hazard chip.
5. Win by attrition — as their team crumbles to status and chip, you simply outlast them. Patience takes it.
